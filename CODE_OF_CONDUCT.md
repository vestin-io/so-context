# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the `so-context` community to be welcoming, respectful, and useful for
everyone.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent while giving clear feedback
- Focus on the work, not the person
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment or intimidation
- Insults, trolling, or sustained hostility
- Publishing private information without permission
- Discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may restrict future
participation when needed to keep the project healthy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers
privately at `info@vestin.io`.
